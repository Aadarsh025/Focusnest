Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How does the Pomodoro timer work? ⌄
The Pomodoro timer divides your work into focused sessions followed by short breaks. You can customize the focus duration, number of sessions, short breaks, and long breaks for each task.
How can I change my environment? ⌄
Open a task and select the Environment option. You can choose from pre-uploaded environments or upload your own background.
How can I upload my own background? ⌄
Go to Environments and select Upload Background. Your uploaded environment will be saved to your personal environment library.
How do I upload music? ⌄
Open the Music section and select Add Music. You can upload supported audio files and use them during your focus sessions.
How do presets work? ⌄
Presets allow you to save a complete focus setup, including timer duration, breaks, sessions, environment and music. You can apply the preset to a task instantly.
